The person behind the code

A little more about me

I choose to study software engineering in 2021 because it was a rapidly evolving field with plenty of opportunities. What started as a practical choice turned into something i genuinely enjoy, from solving problems in code to understanding how teams ang organizations can work together to build better software.

I'm particularly interested in requirements engineering, software testing, and organizational aspects of software development. For me, it is important to understand the context of the software and the people involved in the project. I enjoy exploring how individual decisions, teamwork, and project management shape both the development process and the software we deliver.

Away from the keyboard, you’ll usually find me in the forest with my dog. We spend a lot of time exploring trails, running together, and practising dog training, with varying levels of agreement on what we’re supposed to be doing. I also spend a lot of time in the Norwegian mountains, hiking in summer and skiing in winter.
